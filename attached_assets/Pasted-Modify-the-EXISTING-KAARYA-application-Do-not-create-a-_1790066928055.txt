# 2. SURPLUS DISTRIBUTION METHOD

At the end of the year, distribute the accumulated Year-End Cooperative Surplus Pool to eligible workers based on their number of completed jobs during that year.

Use this formula:

Worker Surplus Share =

(Worker's Completed Jobs ÷ Total Completed Jobs of All Eligible Workers)

× Total Year-End Surplus Pool

Example:

Total Year-End Surplus Pool:

₹1,00,000

Total completed jobs by all eligible workers:

1,000 jobs

Worker A completed:

100 jobs

Worker A's share:

100 ÷ 1,000 × ₹1,00,000

= ₹10,000

Worker B completed:

50 jobs

Worker B's share:

50 ÷ 1,000 × ₹1,00,000

= ₹5,000

Make this calculation dynamic using the demo worker/job data.

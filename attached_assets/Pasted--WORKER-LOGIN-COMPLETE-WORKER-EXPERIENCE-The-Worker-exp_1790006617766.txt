# WORKER LOGIN + COMPLETE WORKER EXPERIENCE

The Worker experience must be a **fully separate, premium application flow** inside KAARYA.

Do not treat the Worker Dashboard as just another page.

The complete flow must be:

**KAARYA Welcome/Login → Worker Login → Worker Dashboard → Job Requests → My Jobs → Earnings → Cooperative Welfare → Insurance & Emergency → Surplus View → Digital Worker Passport → Availability → Ratings → Profile → Safety/SOS**

The Worker experience should be designed specifically for service professionals and should feel simple, trustworthy, empowering and professional.

# 28. IMPORTANT KAARYA CONSISTENCY RULE

Everywhere in the application, maintain the same cooperative model:

For a ₹1,000 service:

**Service Value: ₹1,000**

**Cooperative Contribution: ₹200**

**Worker Earnings: ₹800**

Contribution:

**₹50 — 5% App & Technology Running Cost**

**₹60 — 6% Worker Insurance & Social Security**

**₹40 — 4% Emergency Assistance Fund**

**₹50 — 5% Year-End Cooperative Surplus**

Total:

**20% Cooperative Contribution**

Do not create conflicting percentages or calculations in the Worker Dashboard.

The KM section is for service activity/impact tracking and must not invent a kilometre-based payout rule.
